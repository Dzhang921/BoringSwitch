# Boring Switch — Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** August 9, 2026

Boring Switch does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. Period.

- Your click count, settings, and statistics are stored only on your device.
- The app contains no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party SDKs.
- The only network activity is Apple's own StoreKit, used to process the optional
  one-time premium purchase. Payment is handled entirely by Apple; we never see
  any payment or account information.
- Nothing you do in the app leaves your device.

If you have questions about this policy, open an issue at
https://github.com/Dzhang921/BoringSwitch/issues.
