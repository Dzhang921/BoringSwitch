## How to record the video (2 minutes, on your iPhone)

1. On your iPhone, add Screen Recording to Control Center if it isn't there
   (Settings → Control Center → add Screen Recording).
2. Swipe into Control Center, tap the record button, wait 3 seconds.
3. Do this flow slowly, ~30–45 seconds total:
   - Launch Boring Switch from the Home Screen.
   - Tap the switch several times — show the light turning on/off and the
     lifetime counter going up.
   - Tap **Customize** → pick a different **Style**, a **Material**, and a
     **Color** → tap **Done**. Show the switch changed.
   - Tap the switch a few more times with the new look.
   - Tap **Stats** → scroll to show the statistics and the **Share Certificate
     of Dedication** row → tap Done.
4. Stop the recording (tap the red pill / Control Center).
5. The video saves to Photos. In Resolution Center, use the attachment (paper-
   clip) button to upload it. If it's too large, trim it in Photos first or
   keep it under ~50 MB.

**2. Devices and OS tested**

**3. App functions and target audience**
Boring Switch is a paid entertainment / fidget app. The single screen is a
realistic light switch. Tapping it toggles a light (the screen brightens and
dims) and plays a satisfying click sound with matching haptic feedback. Every
tap is added to a lifetime counter. It is a relaxing, "oddly satisfying" toy —
the target audience is people who enjoy fidget toys, ASMR/soothing sounds, and
minimalist novelty apps. There is no problem it "solves"; its value is a
calming, tactile moment, delivered with a sense of humor.

**4. Setup and accessing main features**
No login, account, or setup is required. All features are available immediately
on launch:
- Tap the switch to flip it (core feature).
- Tap "Customize" (bottom left) to change the switch style, material, and color.
- Tap "Stats" (bottom right) to view click statistics and share a Certificate
  of Dedication image.
- A Home Screen widget shows the lifetime click count.
There are no credentials or sample files needed.

**5. External services / tools**
None. The app is fully self-contained and offline. It uses only Apple's own
system frameworks (SwiftUI, AVFoundation for the sounds, WidgetKit for the
widget, UIKit haptics). There are no third-party SDKs, no analytics, no ads, no
authentication, no payment processors, and no AI services. The app is a paid
app with no in-app purchases.

**6. Regional differences**
None. The app functions identically in all regions. It has no server, no
region-locked content, and no localized behavior differences.

**7. Regulated industry / third-party material**
Not applicable. The app is not in a regulated industry and contains no
protected third-party material. All artwork and sounds were created by the
developer (the click sounds are procedurally generated; the icon and switch
graphics are original).
